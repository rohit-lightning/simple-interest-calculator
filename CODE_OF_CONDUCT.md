
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a friendly,
respectful, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and polite.
- Use friendly and inclusive language.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Help other contributors.
- Respect different opinions.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Offensive language.
- Sharing private information without permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour,
please contact the project maintainers through GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed fairly.
